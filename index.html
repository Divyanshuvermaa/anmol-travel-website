Yamunotri

📍 Uttarkashi District · 3,293 m altitude

First stop of the Char Dham Yatra and the source of the holy Yamuna river. The shrine of Goddess Yamuna sits among snow peaks and is reached by a scenic trek from Janki Chatti.

Best Time
May–Jun, Sep–Oct
Distance
~240 km to Janki Chatti
Highlights
Yamunotri Temple, Surya Kund, Divya Shila
Type
Pilgrimage · Char Dham

✅ Key Tips

  • 6 km trek from Janki Chatti — ponies, palkis and porters available
  • Start early — the trek is easier in the morning
  • Devotees cook rice in the hot spring Surya Kund as prasad
  • Carry warm clothes and rain protection

Gangotri

📍 Uttarkashi District · 3,100 m altitude

Origin of the holy Ganga (as the Bhagirathi). The temple of Goddess Ganga stands beside the river, and the Gaumukh glacier — the river's source — lies a trek beyond.

Best Time
May–Jun, Sep–Oct
Distance
~270 km
Highlights
Gangotri Temple, Bhagirathi River, Gaumukh Trek
Type
Pilgrimage · Char Dham

✅ Key Tips

  • Road is motorable right up to the temple
  • Gaumukh trek (18 km one way) needs a forest permit from Uttarkashi
  • Stay at Harsil for apple orchards and quiet mountain views
  • Carry woollens — nights are cold even in summer

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva and among the holiest shrines of the Char Dham, set below the snow-capped Kedarnath peak beside the Mandakini river.

Best Time
May–Jun, Sep–Oct
Distance
~220 km to Sonprayag
Highlights
Kedarnath Temple, Bhairavnath Temple, Mandakini Valley
Type
Pilgrimage · Char Dham

✅ Key Tips

  • 16 km trek from Gaurikund — ponies, palkis and helicopter service available
  • Complete Char Dham yatra registration before travelling
  • Weather changes fast — carry rain gear and warm clothes
  • Plan for a night stay near Guptkashi or Phata

📍 Chamoli District · above 4,000 m

Sacred Sikh pilgrimage beside a glacial lake ringed by snow peaks. The trek begins at Govindghat and passes through Ghangaria.

Best Time
Jun – Oct
Distance
~295 km to Govindghat
Highlights
Gurudwara Hemkund Sahib, Ghangaria, Glacial Lake
Type
Pilgrimage · Sikh

✅ Key Tips

  • Trek: Govindghat → Ghangaria 13 km, then a steep 6 km climb
  • Ponies, porters and palkis available; helicopter runs Govindghat–Ghangaria
  • Carry light woollens and rain gear
  • Combine with Valley of Flowers from Ghangaria

Vaishno Devi

📍 Trikuta Hills, Katra, Jammu & Kashmir

One of India's most-visited pilgrimages — the shrine of Mata Vaishno Devi sits high in the Trikuta Hills, reached by a trek, pony, palki or helicopter from base camp Katra.

Best Time
Mar – Oct (open year-round; heavy snow in peak winter)
Distance
~600 km from Haridwar (or fly to Jammu)
Highlights
Vaishno Devi Bhawan, Bhairon Temple, Ardhkuwari Cave
Type
Pilgrimage · Shakti Peeth

✅ Key Tips

  • Register for the free Yatra Parchi at Katra before starting the trek
  • 13 km trek from Katra — pony, palki, e-rickshaw and helicopter (Katra–Sanjichhat) all available
  • Battery-operated cars cover part of the route
  • Also visit Bhairon Temple, traditionally after Bhawan darshan

Pushkar

Sacred lake town ringed by 52 ghats and home to one of the world's few Brahma temples, famous worldwide for its annual camel fair.

Best Time
Oct – Mar (Camel Fair around Oct/Nov)

Pilgrimage · Heritage

Yamunotri

📍 Uttarkashi District · 3,293 m altitude

The source of River Yamuna and seat of Goddess Yamuna. Hot water springs (Surya Kund) are used to cook rice offerings.

Open Season
May – Nov
Base Camp
Janki Chatti
Trek Distance
6 km one-way
From Haridwar
~220 km

✅ Key Tips

  • Carry rice & potatoes to cook in Surya Kund
  • Trek starts from Janki Chatti (6 km)
  • Palki & ponies available for elderly
  • Temple opens on Akshaya Tritiya
🏔️

Gangotri

📍 Uttarkashi District · 3,048 m altitude

Origin of River Ganga. The Gangotri temple is dedicated to Goddess Ganga. Gaumukh glacier (actual source) is 18 km further ahead.

Open Season
May – Nov
Base Town
Uttarkashi
Road Access
Direct by road
From Haridwar
~270 km

✅ Key Tips

  • Fill Gangajal for home puja
  • Gaumukh trek needs permit
  • Night temperature drops to 0°C
  • Temple opens on Akshaya Tritiya
🔱

Kedarnath

📍 Rudraprayag District · 3,583 m altitude

One of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Ancient stone temple built by the Pandavas. Surrounded by snow-capped Himalayan peaks.

Open Season
May – Nov
Base Camp
Gaurikund / Sonprayag
Trek Distance
16 km one-way
From Haridwar
~250 km

✅ Key Tips

  • Helicopter available from Phata/Sirsi/Guptkashi
  • Book helicopter 2–3 months in advance
  • Ponies & palki from Gaurikund
  • Biometric registration mandatory at Sonprayag

Badrinath

📍 Chamoli District · 3,133 m altitude

One of the 108 Divya Desams dedicated to Lord Vishnu. Located on the banks of Alaknanda River between Nar and Narayan mountains.

Open Season
May – Nov
Nearby
Mana Village, Tapt Kund
Road Access
Direct by road
From Haridwar
~320 km

✅ Key Tips

  • Take holy dip in Tapt Kund before darshan
  • Visit Mana — last Indian village before Tibet
  • Brahma Kapal — pind-dan for ancestors
  • Temple closes on Diwali day

Anmol Travels was founded with a single mission — to make the sacred Char Dham Yatra safe, comfortable, and affordable for every pilgrim. Based at Haridwar Railway Station, Gate No. 3, we are the first point of contact for thousands of devotees arriving in Haridwar every year.

Our proprietor has 15+ years of personal experience in Uttarakhand pilgrim routes. These terms apply to all packages, taxi services, and helicopter bookings made with Anmol Travels.

1. Booking & Payment Terms

  • Booking is confirmed only after advance payment or verbal/written confirmation.
  • For packages above ₹20,000 — minimum 25% advance is required to confirm.
  • Balance payment to be made before departure or as mutually agreed.
  • Payment accepted via: Cash, UPI (PhonePe / GooglePay / Paytm), NEFT/IMPS.
  • A receipt / booking confirmation will be provided for all advance payments.
  • Prices quoted are per vehicle (not per person) unless stated otherwise.

2. Inclusions & Exclusions

  • Included: Cab/vehicle, experienced driver, fuel, up to 250 km/day (for packages).
  • Excluded: Hotels, meals, porter/palki, helicopter, toll/parking, temple entry fees, personal expenses.
  • Toll & parking charges are extra (approx ₹1,500–₹3,500 for full Char Dham).
  • Extra km beyond package limit charged at ₹12–₹18/km depending on vehicle.
  • Driver night halt charges apply if stay extends beyond itinerary: ₹300–500/night.

3. Route & Itinerary Changes

  • Anmol Travels follows the agreed itinerary unless diverted by weather, road conditions, or government orders.
  • Route changes due to landslides, heavy rain, or road closures are not in our control.
  • In case of route diversion, alternate sightseeing may be provided.
  • Yatra season: May – November. Temple opening/closing dates depend on official announcements.
  • Helicopter service subject to weather and government approval on day of travel.

4. Passenger Responsibilities

  • Carry valid government ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving Licence) for all passengers.
  • Biometric registration is mandatory at Sonprayag for Kedarnath — carry Aadhaar.
  • Senior citizens/patients with serious medical conditions should consult doctor before high-altitude travel.
  • Passengers are responsible for their own belongings. Anmol Travels is not liable for lost items.
  • Smoking, alcohol, and non-vegetarian food are prohibited in vehicles during Yatra.
  • Passengers must be ready at the agreed pickup time. Waiting beyond 30 min may incur extra charges.

5. Driver & Vehicle Policy

  • Our drivers are licensed, experienced, and trained for Himalayan mountain roads.
  • Driver shall not be asked to violate traffic rules or drive on closed/prohibited routes.
  • In case of vehicle breakdown, a replacement vehicle will be arranged at the earliest.
  • Anmol Travels reserves the right to change the vehicle model (similar or upgraded) if required.
